Accounts

Do I need an account?

You can use EduSync with or without an account!

With an account: All your previous files and fact-checking history will be securely stored in our database, accessible from anywhere.

Without an account: History is stored temporarily in your browser and may be lost if you clear data or log out.

Pro tip: You can sign up later from a guest account in settings, and all your browser-stored history will be seamlessly transferred to our database!
